Problem Statement

Why DoubtConnect?

Students often get stuck while studying late at night and post queries in large batch WhatsApp groups. Their messages get drowned under hundreds of chats, and finding someone who can clearly explain the concept feels impossible.

The Chat Group Bottleneck

WhatsApp and Telegram groups are noisy. Questions are overlooked, responses are disorganized, and there is no tracking of whether a doubt was ever resolved.

Verified Peer Matching

Find fellow students who have already mastered your specific course code (INT335, CSE316, CSE304) and are actively available to guide you.

Ratings & Mutual Recognition

Helper students build an academic reputation through star ratings, verified resolved stats, and authentic student reviews.

Simple Workflow

How DoubtConnect Works

From getting stuck on a topic to celebrating resolved understanding in 4 streamlined steps.

1

Post Your Doubt

Specify your course code, subject, topic, and urgency level with detailed problem context.

2

Discover Helpers

Browse available peer mentors filterable by subject proficiency, or receive incoming help offers.

3

Collaborate in Real-Time

Engage in focused 1-on-1 discussion room powered by Socket.IO with typing indicators and instant messaging.

4

Resolve & Rate

Mark doubt as resolved, submit a 5-star rating, and reward your peer explainer with valuable feedback.
