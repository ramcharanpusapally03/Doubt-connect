## 4. Selection Decision & Justification of Rejections

### Winning Concept: DoubtConnect
**Why DoubtConnect Won**:
DoubtConnect scored **4.80/5.00**, dominating across speed, psychological safety, and explainer motivation. It removes the social anxiety of broadcast groups by moving students into focused 1-on-1 real-time Socket.IO discussion rooms, while closing the loop through a 5-star rating and reputation system that genuinely motivates high-performing peer mentors.

### Detailed Analysis of Rejected Concepts
1. **Concept B (Physical Hostel Whiteboard)**:
   - *Reason for Rejection*: Completely inaccessible to day scholars (who represent 30% of LPU students). Requires physical presence in one specific wing; leaves no persistent record of solutions.
2. **Concept C (WhatsApp Group Ticketing Bot)**:
   - *Reason for Rejection*: Fails to eliminate notifications and social clutter. Group members still see tickets intermingled with memes; WhatsApp Cloud API restrictions impose high costs and potential group ban risks.
3. **Concept D (Centralized Faculty Booking)**:
   - *Reason for Rejection*: Overburdens already swamped faculty; fails to address late-night 11:00 PM study bottlenecks when faculty are unavailable.
4. **Concept E (Paid Peer Tutoring Marketplace)**:
   - *Reason for Rejection*: Violates university campus ethics, introduces payment disputes, and creates an unfair economic barrier where students with less money are denied basic academic guidance.
