## 3. Point of View (POV) Statement

Strictly following the mandatory format:
`[Specific User] needs [Real Need] because [Surprising Insight from Evidence]`

> **A focused undergraduate engineering hosteller studying late at night**  
> **NEEDS**  
> **a direct, low-friction channel to discover and connect 1-on-1 with a willing, subject-verified peer explainer within hours**  
> **BECAUSE**  
> **mass broadcast communication channels bury academic queries under social chatter, creating anxiety and social hesitation that force students into late-night isolation and copied coursework, while capable peers willing to teach remain completely unaware of the demand around them.**

---

## 4. Final Problem Statement
*One clear sentence with zero technology or solution named.*

> **Undergraduate students facing late-night coursework difficulties cannot connect with willing peer explainers because unorganized broadcast channels drown academic questions, causing unresolved doubts to compound into academic failure.**

---

## 5. How Might We (HMW) Questions

1. *HMW make finding an available, subject-proficient peer as effortless as checking if a classmate's light is on?*
2. *HMW give peer explainers tangible academic recognition and verified feedback without creating a transactional or monetary barrier?*
3. *HMW transform messy, anxiety-inducing public group doubt discussions into calm, focused 1-on-1 collaborative sessions?*
4. *HMW help students communicate the exact context of their coursework doubt without fear of judgment from faculty or classmates?*

### Selected Main HMW Question (Approved for Checkpoint 3)
> ⭐ **How might we connect a stuck student with a willing peer explainer within hours, not days, while providing mutual academic recognition for both?**
